READ ME FIRST (page added 2026-11-16)

THIS WORKBOOK WAS MADE BEFORE THE REBUILD THAT BEGAN ON 2026-11-16. IT IS KEPT FOR THE RECORD ONLY.
Do not rely on the calculated values in it. A rebuilt workbook will replace it, and this one will then be removed.

WHY ITS VALUES ARE NOT RELIED ON
The values here rest on an earlier calculation foundation that was found to be unsound in these ways:
1. The order book was restarted in the middle of every trade day (at 8:00 pm Eastern), so every resting order's age was wrong after that point.
2. Two of the four trade days, Monday Oct 2 and Tuesday Oct 17, 2023, were missing their first two hours (6:00 pm to 8:00 pm Eastern the evening before). 709 trades were never included.
3. Calculations stepped through the tape one whole second at a time, although the tape is recorded in nanoseconds.
4. In many places only the top 10 price levels of the book were read, not the whole book.
5. Fixed time windows and fixed cut-offs decided ahead of time what was recorded and what was not.
These were found, not chosen. The values were not corrected one by one; they are being recalculated from the beginning.

WHAT THIS WORKBOOK HOLDS
The flow companion table (P01, fresh versus expiring flow). It was built on trade flow added up per whole second and rolled over a fixed 20 seconds, which are two of the faults listed above.

KNOWN GAPS IN THIS WORKBOOK (they are not hidden errors)
- Anything here for trade days Oct 2 and Oct 17, 2023 leaves out the first two hours of those trade days.
- Rows here are trades or selected moments, not every message from the exchange.
- Some columns are empty because they were never calculated for these trade days: the Step 1 columns (an earlier 2021 study, handled separately), the outputs of the Frankie model (not run on these trade days), and the S columns on three of the four trade days.
- Row numbering on trade days Oct 2 and Oct 17 is the old numbering and will not match the rebuilt workbook.

WHAT THE REBUILT WORKBOOK WILL BE
- Built only from the raw order tape and from published outside figures. Nothing is copied from this workbook.
- One row for every message from the exchange (one tick), nothing merged, sampled or left out.
- A trade day runs 6:00 pm Eastern the evening before to 5:00 pm Eastern on the named date. The hour from 5:00 pm to 6:00 pm is marked separately.
- The order book is kept for the whole week, at full depth, and never restarted inside a trade day.
- Every calculated column starts empty and is filled only when its calculation has been rebuilt.

WORDS USED
- Trade day: the exchange's own session, 6:00 pm Eastern the evening before to 5:00 pm Eastern on the named date. A date written alone means a trade day.
- Tick: one single message from the exchange.

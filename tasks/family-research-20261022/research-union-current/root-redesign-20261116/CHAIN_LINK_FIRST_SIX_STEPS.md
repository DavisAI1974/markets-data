# The chain link models: the first six steps back (read-out for Greg, 2026-11-16 evening Eastern)

Read from the stored tables `chain-models-<trade day>-part*.parquet` on the four `root-rows` branches. Nothing here
is a new calculation on the tape: it counts what the pods stored. Every peak's own values are in those tables; the
counts and means below are an addition beside them, never a replacement. The four trade days are kept apart.

What a line says. For one peak, one model and one step back d: the squared error on each of the ten figures of
what the peak did, WITHOUT the d-th ended peak before it (the model given d-1 peaks) and WITH it (given d peaks).
"Gained" means the error with it was smaller. Each trade day was scored by models taught on the other three trade
days, some of them later, so none of this was known at a tick.

To keep in mind when reading it:
- **Some gain is true by definition.** A peak "beaten" means a larger peak came on the same side, and neighbouring
  peaks are cut from the same stretch of the 20-second flow. So the size of the next peak and which side it is on
  follow partly from the peak before by construction. Those figures are shown apart from the rest below.
- A peak given no peak before is said to be the teaching trade days' mean, so the error without at step 1 sits near
  1 on every figure (the figures are standardised).
- Ridge chose the last setting of its list (1000) at most steps and the one before it (100) at the others, and
  nearest neighbours the last of theirs (160) at every step on every trade day. The best setting may lie beyond
  the original's lists. The lists were not changed. Extra trees chose inside its list (leaf 20 or 40, once 10).
- Nearest neighbours and extra trees have no independent recount.

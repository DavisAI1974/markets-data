  any other run only when at least one independent challenge of the current revision is on record.
- [6] The code that hands records over today goes through that rule: `tools/ratchet/file_proposer.py`

## Ticket 05: a check is written after a trial

- `defend check --claim --file <check.json> --run run:NNNN`. [10] The run's role is exactly `reviewer` and it
  registered no revision of the claim. An independent challenge of the revision checked is on record first.
- [9] The check answers the revision named in the release of the trial's records to that run, not whatever is
  current later; each input that is an outcome record must be named in such a release. After a claim's first
  release, a later revision is accepted only with `outcome_seen: true` and is stamped
  `registered_after_outcome_release`.
- The file: `inputs` (`what`, `repo`, `path`, `commit`, hashed by code); one entry for EACH falsifier of that
  revision: `falsifier`, `check_type`, `method`, `method_version`, `location`, `scope`, `what_was_checked`,
  `what_was_observed`, `what_remains_open`, `reproducibility`, `exceptions`, and [11] `observations`: one line
  for each event or cell looked at, each its own: `event`, `seen` (true or false: the falsifier's observation
  was or was not seen in that event) and, when seen, `observed_at` (the input, the lines, the quoted words).
  There is no yes or no for the falsifier as a whole, and code adds nothing up.
- One Evaluation for each falsifier (rubric `falsifier check v1`), `expected_observation` copied by code from
  the registered falsifier, the hashes code computed, the reviewer's run. No verdict field. [12] A quoted line
  (`quote`) is not searched for verdict words. `defend unchecked` lists falsifiers with no check.
- Checks without the engine: a scratch copy of the real graph; the artifact is the real `decider_v1.py`; the
  challenge is by a real reviewer run of the record. Shown on a real trial only on Greg's go (ticket 40).

## Confidence, as the plans place it (his words: "after that it's just scored confidence wise"; "Wverything stays as a.possibility. it's a matter of 0 confidence or highly confident")

[19] [20] [21] The verifier plan puts the promotion of confidence AFTER the verifier, at human-reviewed
disposition with its own approval (its lines 45 and 50), and forbids a model vote, an aggregate score and
automatic promotion (line 78). So: no agent states a confidence at registration, in a challenge or in a check

been contested and every falsifier of that revision has a check. It writes no new revision of the claim. Nothing
is removed for a low confidence; a claim at none stays with everything written about it. The session text shows
the newest disposition beside the claim's place in the process. A strategy's standing is the dispositions of the
claims it rests on, each shown by itself; tying each rule to its claims is tickets 07 and 39.

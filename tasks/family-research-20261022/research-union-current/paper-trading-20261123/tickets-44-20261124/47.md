## CORRECTION (2026-11-24, on his word): the April engine CAN be used on the tape

He said: "The operator can definitely be used. That operator is describing the digital word. Do more resurch and
reading before you throw that away". The lines above that call it "a physics engine" that is "NOT usable on a tape
as it is" and say "its input is not a market's" are WRONG and are corrected here; they were written before the
engine's code was read. The decisions file holds the whole correction. In short:
- `od_engine.py` fits d(state)/dt = L times state for a state that is a 2 by 2 array at each time; nothing in the least-squares fit is physics; it takes any (n_t, 2, 2) array and uneven times; with `n_segments` above 1 it fits one operator for each stretch. The eigenvalues of each L are rates and cycles.
- Bought and sold are the two populations of such a state, and the dipole line is their difference. The natural gas line files hold `bought`, `sold` and `flow` at every read for the four trade days. A rate of L is how fast an imbalance dies; a change of L is a change of regime.
- The May plan was to port this engine to markets; a stand-in was built instead. The engine has not been run on a market, as far as was read.
SO THE PLAN (NEXT, step 1) IS NOW: the engine as it is, given natural gas's two-sided state, as the first method; the trunk's operator basis and symbolic search beside it. His to say in the plan: what fills the two cross terms; which span the populations are read over; how the stretches are cut; the go and its budget. The guards stay: an independent held-out test (April's was not), stretches not cut blind to the data, the leakage gate, each event on its own, the four trade days.

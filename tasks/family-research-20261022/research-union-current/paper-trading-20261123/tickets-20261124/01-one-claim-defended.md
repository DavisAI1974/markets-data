# 01: One claim defended, end to end (the tracer bullet)

**What to build:** One claim of the first written decider (its divergence condition) goes through the whole of the independent verifier's path, short of a trial: it is registered before any outcome is seen, an independent reviewer challenges it without seeing the proposer's reasoning, and both are on record in the graph where one command shows them. It proves the shape every later claim will take.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The claim is a node of the graph carrying the registration contract's fields: proposer, hypothesis, exact claim, scope, mechanism, alternative explanations, falsifiers written beforehand, the code commit and the file's hash, and that no outcome has been seen
- [ ] The reviewer's brief holds the exact claim and the admissible evidence and NOT the proposer's mechanism or reasons
- [ ] The challenge is an Evaluation node naming its rubric, with at least one competing explanation, one falsifier, and the observation that would tell them apart; what was checked, what was observed, what remains open; no verdict word
- [ ] One command prints the claim, what it rests on, its challenge and what is open
- [ ] Missing pieces covered: A1, A2, A3, A11

NG Dipole Renders
The dipole work of the October discovery pass, drawn from the committed summaries on
codex/ng-shadow-discovery-20261108: the frozen roll-20 detector on four sessions (October
2, 3, 17 and 18, 2023), its clocks, its states, and what they do and do not carry. Every figure
has its numbers in a table below it. Hover a mark for its value. No trade cost enters anything
shown.

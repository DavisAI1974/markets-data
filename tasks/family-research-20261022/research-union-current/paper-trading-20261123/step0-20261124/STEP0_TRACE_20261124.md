## What did not match, and what was done

**CL14 (fixed, Claude's; Greg's to strike).** Text: "the FIRST sell since its latest step prints at the level that
step made". The code did not count the print whose own quote showed the step. The quote a print carries stood
BEFORE its trade: on the 10-minute recording all 55 buys print at their own offer and all 43 sells at their own
bid; on the 25-minute recording 74 of 74 buys and 224 of 225 sells. So when a sell print's own bid is higher than
the print before, the bid stepped up first and that sell is the first one since. Fixed in `4142f97`
(`tasty/paper/rules_cl.py`), with a check that reads the raw messages and fails on the old code
(`tests/tasty_runner/test_paper_replay.py`, the fourth check). CL14 now opens 23 times on the 10-minute recording
(14 before) and 60 on the 25-minute one (37 before). Every line of the other rules is unchanged, compared line by
line. All four checks pass on both recordings.

**Closes shown by a print with no side (not fixed yet; point c).** A print with no side is never given to a rule.
A quote step or the end of a trade leg that such a print shows reaches the rule at the next print WITH a side. The
ledger then writes that later print as where the trade closed, with that later print's bid and offer, and no mark.
Confirmed by hand on the 10-minute recording:
- CL06-0005: the bid stepped down at idx 30 (no side; bid 3.537, offer 3.541). The ledger says closed at idx 32,
  bid 3.540, offer 3.541: the same two prices it opened at, because the bid had stepped back up by then.
- CL06-0016: the step was at idx 96 (no side); written at idx 97.
- CL06-0017 and CL14-0014: the offer stepped up at idx 104 (no side; offer 3.534); written at idx 105, offer 3.533.
The verdicts are as the text says. The place and the two quotes on the line are not the tick the rule names.
The 25-minute recording has 141 prints with no side of 440, so it happens more there; not counted.
Rule 5 of "what holds for all fifteen" says: "A trade the tape gives no side for neither opens nor closes
anything." On ROOT's tape a quote step is its own row; on this feed it rides on a print.

**The count of gaps (not fixed yet; point d).** The builder counts a gap each time the feed's day volume grows by
more than the sizes of the prints seen. Measured on the raw messages: on the 10-minute recording that shortfall
grew 15 times and SHRANK 12 times; on the 25-minute one it grew 87 times and shrank 52. A print the feed never
sent cannot come back, so a shortfall that shrinks is a day-volume message arriving before its prints, not a gap.
At the end the day volume is ahead of the prints by 21 lots (10 minutes) and 81 lots (25 minutes): that much is
real. The prints' own sequence number never shows a hole: it steps by 1 (72 times on the two recordings, every
one the same feed time, side and price as the print before) or by 2 (every other time), never more.

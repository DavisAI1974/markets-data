# What paper trading must settle

All fifteen rules have an observation and an action. These questions remain open for each individual paper occurrence.

- **CX01–CX04.** How much price movement remains after q has arrived? Capture the first distinct print after q, each intervening print, both quotes, the two line values and the actual tick that ends the idea. A move already completed between p and q cannot answer that question.
- **CX05–CX06.** Did the flow turn and threatened quote identify a useful exit on an open paper position, and how early? The missing inputs are a prospective position identifier, direction, open position at p and q, decision receive time and subsequent tape. The four development tapes contain no paper-position history.
- **CX07–CX10.** When one quote moves first, does the opposite quote follow, stay put or go against it? Preserve each quote revision, intervening prints and exact sequence positions after q. Endpoint equality at two prints leaves the intervening quote path open.
- **CX11–CX12.** Does the tested bid or offer continue to support an existing position after an opposing aggressor trades there? Log every subsequent quote and the management state on that paper position. A single trade at a level does not establish how long liquidity will remain there.
- **CX13–CX14.** Does pausing an addition give an earlier warning than the later exit condition? Log whether an addition was actually pending, when the warning cleared or converted to an exit, and what the print and each quote did before then. Where no addition was pending, retain the warning as information.
- **CX15.** At an unchanged print price and unchanged quote endpoints, is the next price change in the direction of the new exhaustion sign? Preserve expiry reads and aggressor messages separately. Participation at q does not by itself identify how much of the sign change came from older trades leaving the span.
- **Across all fifteen.** The four October 2023 trade days are the whole discovery record. No claim about other contracts or market conditions follows. The missing evidence for performance is the unseen natural gas paper feed, ordered tape positions and nanosecond clocks, plus paper state for the management rules. Recheck prefix invariance on the paper adapter using real tape truncations before relying on its decisions. No broker, paper adapter, credentials or live state was supplied or started in this task.

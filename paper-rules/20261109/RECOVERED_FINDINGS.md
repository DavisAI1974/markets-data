# Findings recovered during reconciliation

These are coverage and mechanism findings from existing producer code and retained evidence. They are not newly measured profits, paper results or claims of predictive superiority.

1. **The eleven initial variants omit two recent model arms.** The canonical reanalysis defined counts/price/book, information, display, and combined additions. Its saved M0/M1 deployment evidence covers the first two. The display and combined arms remain included, with missing frozen coefficients recorded. See [exact source bindings](supplemental_reanalysis_models.json).
2. **The saved selling condition needs the full book.** Recent selling plus 60-second buying is only part of the source screen; bid depth also had to exceed ask depth. The exact buying inverse and the flow-only buying mirror are different hypotheses. See [FC definitions](review_october_recipes.json).
3. **Weakening has several observable causes.** Expiring old volume, fresh opposing flow, vanishing flow and within-window changes are separately defined. Ten continuous predicates were recovered, including episodes with no subsequent birth. They must survive into paper testing independently of whether a later family label appears. See the [original producer](review_sources/research_atlas_continuous.py).
4. **Some order context is already known at onset.** Fill-linked reduction and next displayed level in the one-lot source are measured at the onset group close. They are eligible causal inputs when their original messages are available; later episode fate remains an outcome. This corrects the earlier blanket warning about those fields.
5. **Chain prose and producer code use different anchors.** The documented structural-onset clock and the producer's endpoint-confirmation clock differ by two seconds. Historical means belong to their actual producing clock. The two source versions are preserved separately, and no result is transferred to the other clock. See [chain review](review_chain.json).
6. **An event label can be known after its apparent timestamp.** The canonical peak detector uses whole-day thresholds and retrospective peak selection. A seed uses its completed future window. Neither identity can be backdated into an earlier paper entry. Exact original definitions remain preserved; delayed causal designs are separately labelled.
7. **More model records do not imply more independent strategies.** The recovered 4,581 task keys include model families, source versions, folds, horizons and references. Missing original model state is a real dependency; copying another model's coefficients would change the hypothesis. See [broad model evidence](review_broad.json).
8. **Midpoint flow and exchange aggressor flow are separate inputs.** High agreement does not make them interchangeable. Each rule binds its original feature definition. Likewise, aggregate ladder changes cannot prove native same-order lifecycle events.
9. **Original quote calculations are not platform fills.** A source within-second price sort can reorder the tape, and source quote-crossing outcomes do not establish an execution. Platform paper reports will supply fill prices; source results remain historical evidence with their limitations attached.
10. **Some missing model state can be recovered exactly without fitting.** The original full-precision table and producer assignments recover 117 constant references: 54 means, 9 frequencies and 54 zeros. The presentation table's rounded values would change 47 resulting binary64 outputs. Original precision is retained; 54 missing median references are not replaced with means. See [constant recovery](machine/CONSTANT_MODEL_STATE_README.md).

Every candidate remains in the paper universe. Missing inputs, absent model state, unresolved source direction, invalidated signals, no fills and losses remain visible. None is an observation-only class or a performance exclusion. The testing process is common; the hypotheses' distinct triggers, targets and horizons remain distinct.

# Uniform paper-testing protocol

All hypotheses receive the same handling. No ranking, confidence threshold, historical profitability screen, winner-only fitting, or observation-only promotion path decides whether a candidate gets a paper trial. Each source-defined variant and its contrary cases are retained. Correcting a proven arithmetic or clock error updates the active value and preserves the original in history; it does not revive a known wrong value as a competing scientific claim.

Every variant uses the same instrument/feed span, order-size policy, execution adapter, arrival-clock accounting, fill authority and outcome reporting. The rule's original trigger and intended holding horizon remain part of its identity. Different source-defined horizons are not silently collapsed into one convenient horizon. Any new paper-protocol choice is identified in the decision ledger and applies symmetrically to the affected comparison.

Rules evaluate at the first complete observation where all required inputs were actually known. A signal cannot use its future outcome, a retrospective family identity, a whole-day quantile computed after the decision, or a future path extreme. Record input-unavailable, zero-direction, missing, censored, invalidated, failed-confirmation, expired, no-fill and adapter-rejected events for every rule. These outcomes remain in each candidate's denominator and register. Missing inputs do not remove candidates.

Quantiles/classifiers/models must have a frozen training cutoff preceding the paper span. If a source's completed-data regime labels were not causal at its original entry, retain that historical label as an outcome; a new causal operating variant requires separately declared earlier-trained thresholds or a decision after the state becomes available. No silently substituted proxy feed, model or detector.

Keep independent attribution for opposing or overlapping strategies. Account-level netting must not make one rule close another rule's trade. Use separate simulated accounts or deterministic separately replayed trials of the same recorded span with the same parameters for all candidates. A synthesized internal fill is not a platform paper fill. Every entry and exit result must come from the target platform's execution report.

The existing risk settings remain a versioned source policy; applying them to newly recovered hypotheses is a declared paper-design choice, not evidence that the original research tested those exits. Do not let an operational cap silently erase triggered signals or treat unfilled signals as zero-profit trades. Keep the full event ledger even when position, session or margin limits prevent submission.

No live orders. Existing research workers and paused migration jobs remain untouched. Logic acceptance tests may verify code; no historical strategy backtest is presented as the requested platform paper trial.

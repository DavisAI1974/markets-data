# Four-day research population

Current workbook: `Frankie_four_day_research_current.xlsx`.

This is an in-progress population checkpoint, not a declaration that every research source is pinned. It retains the existing review workbook and adds all 143,270 saved trade clocks, descriptive saved-lifecycle associations for every trade, the 20 corrected price-response examples, screenshot-to-source links, and the underlying tables for the ten illustrated NG cases. Trading session and UTC tape member are separate identities. Historical Bitcoin/crypto images are conceptual attachments only.

The 20 price-response examples have separate exact scheduled cutoffs and source-observed endpoint receive clocks. All 80 clock cells were read back from the saved XLSX and matched against the corrected integer source values. Nanosecond clocks are text to preserve precision. The original erroneous outcomes are not active alternatives.

The verified companion batch contains 5,075,640 timestamp-indexed research rows in 23 Excel files and all 143,270 saved trades with their 141 original layer fields in 17 Excel files. Each research row includes complete source fields, exact clock text, source row identity and its immutable source link. The master workbook's Excel companion index lists the files. Long layer payloads are split into complete hash-verified chunks, without truncation. These are the saved trade universe, not a claim of complete coverage of subsequently recovered longer tapes.

Original SQLite recovery added 1,845 source artifacts. Their 34,825,917 parsed records comprise 5,569,627 clock-indexed rows and 29,256,290 comparison, aggregate or currently unbound rows. Their Excel export is still running. Another 448 four-day workflow artifacts are being processed. These unfinished exports are not counted in the verified companion batch. Non-event studies and unbound records retain complete immutable source attachments in published_source_catalog.json. No performance-based exclusion is applied. A source catalog entry alone does not mean its data have been joined to a trade.

An additional 6,832 source records had generated horizon clocks calculated from session origin instead of event origin. Their corrected active versions use event t0 plus the producer's duration, with observed endpoints kept separate. The four corrected evidence files and correction basis are included. Faulty values are not retained as active alternatives.

All 50 original CCode render views were inspected through the ten five-view case composites. ccode_render_review.csv connects each original view to its composite and the full underlying Excel tables. Case 4 confirmation at +675 seconds exceeds its displayed +600-second lifecycle window; this remains an explicit coverage gap, not a reason to discard the case.

`research_gap_queue.csv` and `CCODE_GAP_FILLER_HANDOFF.md` describe the remaining work. The exact original extraction registry contains all 19 second-set field identities; that does not prove full coverage on other dates. The two user-requested workflow calculation families still require exact run/output reconciliation. The new NG dipole-dive calculation is queued, not reported as completed.

The saved lifecycle association is descriptive, uses the producer's labeled ending convention, and does not establish that the future ending was knowable at the trade. Missing mappings remain explicit. All eight lifecycle source tables were verified against their pinned Git blobs.

No new remote compute job, live trade, merge, or deployment was launched for this checkpoint.
